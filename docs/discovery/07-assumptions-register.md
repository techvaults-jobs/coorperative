# 7. Assumptions Register

None of these statements may be treated as fact. Each has a test, and an owner records its status: **Untested**, **Supported**, **Refuted** or **Partly true** (with the conditions).

| # | Assumption | Why it is risky if wrong | How we test it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Workplace cooperatives (segments A–D) share one operating core | We could build the wrong core, or four products | Compare rule matrices across ≥6 workplace societies | Untested |
| A2 | Payroll deduction is the main collection channel for workplace societies | The reconciliation module could be wrongly prioritised | Interview count; deduction file samples | Untested |
| A3 | Societies are willing to pay for software, and someone holds a budget | No business | Ask for current spend and who decides; test price anchors | Untested |
| A4 | Officers would trust a cloud system with member financial data | Adoption fails | Interviews; ask about previous software experiences | Untested |
| A5 | Members want self-service on their phones | Member app over-invested | Member interviews; device survey | Untested |
| A6 | Interest is usually flat-rate on the original principal | Wrong calculation engine | Collect D12 schedules from ≥6 societies | Untested |
| A7 | Guarantors are always other members | Guarantor model wrong | D10 forms | Untested |
| A8 | A member's savings can be offset against their loan at exit | Settlement errors, legal disputes | Bye-laws + legal review | **Partly supported:** Act s.16 gives the society a charge and set-off over a member's shares, deposits and dividends; order and scope still need bye-laws + counsel |
| A9 | Every society pays dividends | Unnecessary module, wrong accounting | Interviews + D17, D19 | Untested |
| A10 | The 25% reserve-fund rule applies to all societies | Wrong distribution logic | Legal review of Act + state laws | **Supported for the federal Act** (s.34(2), with Director exemption possible); state laws not yet checked |
| A11 | State laws largely mirror the federal Act | Per-state rules missed | Obtain 3 state laws; counsel review | Untested. Note: the minimum-membership figure differs (Act s.3: 10; guides: 20 for savings and credit) |
| A12 | Cooperatives taking member savings need no CBN licence, and our platform needs none | Regulatory exposure | Counsel opinion | Untested |
| A13 | Employers will accept a standard deduction file format from us | Integration effort underestimated | Payroll officer interviews; D06, D07 samples | Untested |
| A14 | IPPIS or state platforms allow cooperatives to submit or receive deduction data digitally | Government segment blocked | Interview MDA payroll staff; official guidance | Untested |
| A15 | Societies have clean opening balances that can be migrated | Pilot stalls on data cleanup | Data-quality review of anonymised registers | Untested |
| A16 | Officers have reliable internet and laptops | Offline needs ignored | Technology questions in §4.9 | Untested |
| A17 | NCSR will expose an API or data standard we can integrate with | Wasted integration work | Ask FMAFS, Seamfix, CFN | Untested |
| A18 | The NCSR revalidation creates demand for digital records | Wrong go-to-market timing | Ask societies whether revalidation pushes them to digitise | Untested |
| A19 | One person per society can authorise configuration changes | Governance mismatch | Governance interviews | Untested |
| A20 | Retirement and exit settlement is a major pain point | Mis-prioritised roadmap | Pain ranking from officers and retired members | Untested |
| A21 | Societies will let TechVaults host data, not insist on on-premise | Architecture mismatch | Interviews; ask about IT policy at parent institutions | Untested |
| A22 | Member counts are as large as press reports suggest (30k vs 370k societies conflict) | Market sizing wrong | Request data from FMAFS and CFN | Untested |
| A23 | A rules engine flexible enough for all segments can stay simple to configure | Product becomes unusable | Prototype configuration UI tested with officers | Untested |
