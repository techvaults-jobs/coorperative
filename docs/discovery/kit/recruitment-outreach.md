# Recruitment Outreach

**Target:** contact about 15 societies to secure 8–12 interviews (D-003). Channels: personal and alumni networks, employer HR contacts, the Cooperative Federation of Nigeria (CFN) and state federations, and State Directors of Cooperatives.

**Tracker columns:** Society ID · Segment · State · Contact role · Channel · Date contacted · Response · Screener done · Interview dates · Documents promised.

## 1. Email or letter to a cooperative society

**Subject:** Research on cooperative operations: request for a 1-hour conversation

Dear [Chairperson / Secretary / Treasurer],

TechVaults is studying how cooperative societies in Nigeria run their day-to-day operations: membership, monthly contributions, loans, accounts, and member retirement or exit. We want to understand the real work and its challenges **before** designing any software.

We would be grateful for one-hour conversations with two or three of your officers, for example the treasurer, the loan officer and the secretary, at a time and place convenient to you. We are not selling anything at this stage.

- Your society and officers will not be named in anything we publish without written permission.
- We will not ask for members' personal information. If you choose to share forms or reports, blank or anonymised copies are all we need.
- You will receive a summary of our findings and early access to whatever we build.

If you're willing, please reply with a suitable contact and some possible dates between [date] and [date].

Yours sincerely,
[Name], [Role], TechVaults
[Phone] · [Email]

## 2. Letter to CFN or a State Director of Cooperatives

**Subject:** Request for guidance and introductions: cooperative operations research

Dear [Title and Name],

TechVaults is conducting research into the operations of cooperative societies across institution types (universities, government agencies, hospitals, private companies, community and multipurpose societies). The research will inform a cooperative management platform that supports good governance, accurate records and transparent reporting, in line with the ongoing national cooperative digitalisation programme.

We respectfully request:
1. A short meeting to understand the [Federation's / Department's] priorities, including registration, audit and returns requirements, and the current revalidation exercise.
2. Copies of any public guidance, model bye-laws, or audit and return formats.
3. Where appropriate, introductions to societies that might take part in interviews.

We will share an anonymised summary of our findings with the [Federation / Department].

Yours faithfully,
[Name], [Role], TechVaults
