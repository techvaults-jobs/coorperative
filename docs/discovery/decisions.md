# Discovery Decision Log

These decisions were accepted by TechVaults on **17 October 2026** ("proceed with every recommended professional decision"). Each one can be revisited when evidence contradicts it. Record the reversal here; don't delete the entry.

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | **Research before build.** No product code, schema or stack lock-in until the Discovery Report (week 12) is accepted. | Master prompt §16 | Discovery Report accepted |
| D-002 | Run the **12-week discovery** starting **2 Nov 2026**, ending about **29 Jan 2027** ([08](08-timeline-and-success-criteria.md)). | Finishes before the NCSR revalidation window closes (about Feb 2027) | Recruitment falls behind by more than 2 weeks |
| D-003 | Sample **8–12 societies, weighted to workplace (payroll) cooperatives**, with at least one community or agricultural and one multipurpose society, across **Lagos, the FCT and one other state** ([01 §1.4](01-cooperative-models.md)). | Likely MVP segment; avoid a payroll-only core by accident | Fewer than 6 workplace societies recruited by week 4 |
| D-004 | Retain three advisers in weeks 1–2: **cooperative and financial-services lawyer**, **ICAN-qualified cooperative auditor**, **NDPA data protection officer or licensed compliance organisation**. Terms of reference in [kit/](kit/). | Several requirements are blocked on their answers ([02 §2.6](02-regulatory-landscape-and-sources.md#26-questions-requiring-expert-review)) | n/a |
| D-005 | **First pilot profile:** a mid-sized (300–2,000 members) single-employer **private-company or university staff cooperative** with savings and 2–4 loan products. **Exclude IPPIS-based federal MDA societies** from the first pilot ([10](10-pilot-selection.md)). | Structured monthly cycle; IPPIS coordination issues reported (02 F27); bank staff coops are proven buyers (06 §6.4) | Discovery evidence shows a better-fitting segment |
| D-006 | Treat the following as **non-configurable guardrails** pending counsel confirmation: 25% statutory reserve with Director exemption only (s.34(2)); education fund ≤10% (s.34(3)); 1/5 share-holding cap (s.27); no loans to non-members (s.30); nominee-first death settlement (s.18). | Statute text verified ([02 F2, F17–F25](02-regulatory-landscape-and-sources.md)) | Counsel memo or state law says otherwise |
| D-007 | Configurability uses **parameters and named, accountant-verified strategies**, not a free-form formula language ([09 §9.2](09-findings-to-prd.md)). | Testable, explainable to auditors | A real society needs a rule no strategy can express |
| D-008 | The platform is a **record-keeping and administration system**. It does **not hold or move funds or take deposits**. Any money movement goes through CBN-licensed providers, after legal review. | CBN deposit-taking restrictions (02 F15); Act s.32 | Counsel opinion received |
| D-009 | **Data minimisation:** no NIN, BVN or bank account numbers are collected during research. In the product, NIN or CoopID storage is optional and DPIA-justified. | NDPA 2023 / GAID 2025 | DPO memo received |
| D-010 | **UI direction (provisional):** shadcn/ui + Radix + Tailwind; TanStack Table and Query; AG Grid only for spreadsheet-grade reconciliation; React Hook Form + Zod; Storybook ([11](11-ux-and-ui-direction.md)). Prototypes in weeks 10–11 use it. | Owned components, accessibility, premium feel | Stage 4 stack decision |
| D-011 | Discovery documents live in this repository (`docs/discovery/`) under version control. A published read-only page is shared with stakeholders. | Single source of truth, change history | n/a |
| D-012 | **Build starts in parallel with discovery (17 Oct 2026; partly supersedes D-001).** At TechVaults' direction, engineering starts on the **policy-independent foundation**: project scaffold, design system, authentication and MFA, multi-society tenancy and isolation, roles and permissions, append-only audit log, double-entry ledger core, and a basic member registry. **Policy-dependent modules** (loan rules, deduction allocation order, exit settlement, dividends) are still gated on discovery evidence and expert memos. They are built only as configurable strategies once the rules are validated. | The foundation is needed whatever the findings. Building policy logic before validation risks encoding the wrong rules (master prompt §§6–7) | Discovery findings contradict a foundation assumption |
| D-013 | **Stack (supersedes the "provisional" status of D-010):** TypeScript end to end; **Next.js (App Router)** web app; **PostgreSQL** with row-level security for tenant isolation; **Drizzle ORM** and SQL migrations; money stored as **integer kobo (bigint)** with documented rounding; **Vitest** for domain tests and **Playwright** for end-to-end tests; pnpm. One deployable modular monolith, no microservices. | Widely known and maintainable; Postgres gives transactional integrity and RLS; integer minor units avoid float errors | Team capability or hosting constraints say otherwise |
| D-014 | **Authentication:** Better Auth with email + password and TOTP two-factor; no self sign-up (societies invite officers); officers with any financial or records role must enable two-factor before opening society data; 8-hour sessions; rate-limited sign-in. Society membership is resolved through a narrow security-definer function so row-level security stays on. | Master prompt §§8–9 (MFA, least privilege) | Discovery shows members need a different sign-in (e.g. phone/OTP) |
| D-015 | **Maker-checker on every journal entry.** No role posts directly: an officer prepares an entry or a reversal, and it reaches the ledger only when a *different* officer with `ledger.approve` (principal officer or chairperson) approves it. Rejections carry a reason; the preparer can withdraw. The database enforces it too (an entry can only be inserted for an approved submission whose approver isn't the preparer). Entry numbers are assigned at approval, **per society with no gaps**. Entries posted before this change keep their original numbers; history is append-only, so earlier gaps stay. | Master prompt §8 separation of duties; auditors query numbering gaps | Discovery shows societies want a value threshold below which a single officer may post (would be a configurable, approved policy) |
| D-016 | **Officers join by invitation link.** An officer with `officers.manage` (secretary or principal officer) creates a single-use link that expires in 7 days and sends it themselves, because there's no email service yet. Only a SHA-256 hash of the token is stored. Officers can't change or remove their own roles. **Known risk:** one person can still invite a second account they control and approve their own entries. Before real use, role grants that include an approval permission should need a second officer's approval, and invitations should be sent by verified email. | Unblocks maker-checker without an email provider | Email provider chosen; before the first pilot society |
| D-017 | **Product name and design system.** The product is **Cooperative Vaults**, a TechVaults product. The UI is an owned component set (no component library dependency) on design tokens derived from one brand colour, with light and dark themes, a validated chart palette and WCAG AA contrast. This supersedes the shadcn/Radix suggestion in D-010; the Tailwind direction stands. | TechVaults direction (17 Oct 2026): "the most advanced and appealing user interfaces" | A component library is needed for complex widgets (data grids, date ranges) |
| D-018 | **Sector-configurable, without code changes.** Each society picks a sector profile (workplace, agricultural, transport, market, multipurpose, thrift & credit, housing, other). The profile sets default terminology (e.g. "Staff ID" → "Farmer ID") and modules; the society can override both, and choose a brand colour that must reach 4.5:1 contrast with white. Settings changes are audited with before/after values. Financial rules are **not** part of a sector profile; they stay in approved product policies (D-007). | Product must be customisable "for any sector or society" | Discovery shows a sector needs structural differences, not just words and modules |
| D-019 | **Loans and products built now, as configurable, approved policies (partly supersedes the gate in D-012).** Savings, share and loan products carry a versioned policy; a new product or a policy change takes effect only when an officer with `settings.approve` who did not propose it approves it. Loan interest uses three named strategies (flat; reducing balance with equal instalments; reducing balance with equal principal), rounded half-up per instalment with the last absorbing remainders. Each loan snapshots the policy it was granted under. Applications must pass every policy rule; a different officer decides them; disbursements and repayments are journal submissions approved by another officer. Repayments cover interest due first, then principal. **All rates, limits and the interest-first allocation are defaults pending the auditor's and counsel's review**; exit settlement, dividends and payroll allocation order remain gated. | TechVaults direction (17 Oct 2026): build a full-featured product | Auditor or counsel memo changes a rule; discovery shows societies need a strategy not offered |
