# 4. Workflow Checklist

Use this checklist **for each society** to map its current-state workflows. For each item, record:

- **Who** does it (role), **when** (trigger and frequency), **how** (tool or channel), **rule** (the policy, with a source document ref), **approval** (who signs off), **exceptions**, and **pain** (H/M/L).
- Mark each item **Confirmed** (seen in a document or demonstrated), **Described** (told to us only), or **Not applicable**.

Items marked ⚖️ need legal or accounting confirmation before we encode them.

## 4.1 Membership

- [ ] Who is eligible: employees only, contract staff, retirees, spouses, external members?
- [ ] Member categories (ordinary, associate, honorary, corporate) and their rights
- [ ] Application channel (paper form, online, HR onboarding pack)
- [ ] Required documents (ID, staff ID, passport photo, payslip, NIN/BVN?) and the reason each is required ⚖️ (NDPA minimisation)
- [ ] Employment verification: who confirms, and how
- [ ] Next-of-kin and nominee details: how many, can they change, how is a change witnessed?
- [ ] Entrance fee, initial shares, minimum first contribution
- [ ] Approval: who approves, and is there a waiting period?
- [ ] Member number format and assignment rules
- [ ] Deduction authority form signed for the employer
- [ ] Welcome communication and passbook or card issued
- [ ] Changes: department, salary, grade, name (marriage), bank account, contact details. Who may edit, and is the change approved?
- [ ] Duplicate detection: how a returning or duplicate member is spotted
- [ ] Suspension: grounds, effect on deductions and loans, reactivation
- [ ] Transfer: to another branch, employer or society; what moves with the member
- [ ] Revalidation data: does the society hold what NCSR and CoopID will need? ⚖️

## 4.2 Savings, shares and contributions

- [ ] List every scheme: share capital, ordinary savings, special or target savings, fixed deposits, thrift, welfare, building fund, Christmas savings, *ajo/esusu*
- [ ] For each scheme: compulsory or voluntary; fixed amount, percentage of salary or member-chosen; minimum and maximum; when it can change
- [ ] Withdrawable or not; notice period; partial withdrawals; penalties
- [ ] Does the scheme earn interest or a return? How is it computed and when credited? ⚖️
- [ ] Share capital: par value, maximum holding, transfer between members, refund on exit ⚖️ (IFRIC 2)
- [ ] Contribution change requests: form, approval, effective payroll month
- [ ] Voluntary lump-sum payments by transfer or cash: receipting, posting
- [ ] Member statements: frequency, format, delivery

## 4.3 Payroll deductions (monthly cycle)

- [ ] Calendar: cut-off for changes, schedule submission date, payroll run, remittance date, reconciliation deadline
- [ ] Building the deduction schedule: savings, shares, loan repayments, fees, special levies per member
- [ ] File format and submission channel to the employer (Excel template, IPPIS upload, email, portal)
- [ ] Net-pay floor or statutory cap check, and who performs it
- [ ] Remittance: single lump sum or itemised; bank account; proof
- [ ] Returned deduction report: what the employer sends back
- [ ] Reconciliation: expected vs actual per member; handling of **short**, **zero**, **excess**, **duplicate**, **unknown member**, and **late** deductions
- [ ] Allocation order when a deduction is partial (fees, then interest, then principal, then savings?) ⚖️
- [ ] Arrears carried forward, member notified, manual top-up allowed
- [ ] Multiple employers or payroll sources per society
- [ ] Non-payroll members: bank transfer, cash, agent collection; receipts and banking of cash

## 4.4 Loans

- [ ] Product catalogue: name, purpose, min/max amount, term, rate, **interest method** (flat, reducing balance, upfront deduction, none), fees, insurance
- [ ] Eligibility rules: months of membership, savings multiple (e.g. 2× savings), salary capacity, number of concurrent loans, years to retirement, no arrears
- [ ] Application: form, supporting documents, channel
- [ ] Guarantors: number, eligibility, maximum guarantee exposure, consent capture, release on repayment
- [ ] Use of the borrower's savings as collateral or lien
- [ ] Approval chain: thresholds by amount; conflict-of-interest rules for officers' loans
- [ ] Waiting list and rationing when funds are short
- [ ] Disbursement: method, deductions at source (fees, insurance, upfront interest), offset of an existing loan
- [ ] Repayment schedule generation; start month
- [ ] Repayment allocation; overpayment handling
- [ ] Top-up and refinancing; rescheduling; moratorium
- [ ] Early repayment: interest rebate or not
- [ ] Arrears ageing; reminders; escalation; recovery from guarantors and from savings ⚖️
- [ ] Write-off approval and later recovery ⚖️
- [ ] Loan register and portfolio reports (portfolio at risk, ageing)

## 4.5 Accounting and financial management

- [ ] Chart of accounts: who designed it, and is it state-prescribed? ⚖️
- [ ] Books kept: cashbook, general ledger, members' ledgers, loan ledgers, fixed-asset register
- [ ] Receipts and payment vouchers: numbering, approval, signatories
- [ ] Journals: who prepares, who approves
- [ ] Bank accounts and reconciliation frequency
- [ ] Interest income recognition; fee income ⚖️
- [ ] Expenses and budgets
- [ ] Investments (fixed deposits, shares, real estate) and trading stock
- [ ] Month-end and year-end close; locking of periods
- [ ] Financial statements produced; framework used ⚖️
- [ ] Corrections: reversal entries vs editing; audit trail
- [ ] Do the member sub-ledgers reconcile to the GL control accounts?

## 4.6 Dividends and surplus

- [ ] Is a distribution made at all? On what (shares, savings, loan interest paid = patronage)?
- [ ] Statutory reserve fund and education fund appropriations ⚖️ (see [02 F2](02-regulatory-landscape-and-sources.md))
- [ ] Proposal by committee; approval at AGM; ministry or auditor clearance ⚖️
- [ ] Eligibility (membership on a cut-off date, minimum balance, no arrears)
- [ ] Calculation basis (year-end balance, average balance, monthly product)
- [ ] Payment: cash, credit to savings, offset against loans
- [ ] Unclaimed dividends

## 4.7 Retirement, resignation, transfer, death, expulsion

Map **each event type separately**.

- [ ] Trigger: notice from member, HR or next of kin; required evidence (retirement letter, resignation acceptance, death certificate)
- [ ] Notice periods; stop-deduction instruction to payroll
- [ ] Confirm the final contribution received
- [ ] Outstanding loans, interest to date, arrears
- [ ] Offset order: loans netted against savings and shares? ⚖️
- [ ] The member's guarantees on others' loans: release, substitution or retention
- [ ] Pending transactions and claims (welfare, levies, unposted deductions)
- [ ] Share refund rules (immediate, after AGM, after a notice period) ⚖️
- [ ] Pro-rata dividend or surplus entitlement for the part year
- [ ] Clearance sign-offs (loan officer, treasurer, secretary, chairperson)
- [ ] Death: nominee vs next of kin vs estate; documents; insurance or welfare benefit ⚖️
- [ ] Settlement computation sheet; approval; payment; acknowledgement
- [ ] Shortfall: what happens if loans exceed savings (guarantors, terminal benefits, legal action)?
- [ ] Final statement; closing the account; record retention; rejoining rules
- [ ] Typical elapsed time and complaints

## 4.8 Governance and audit

- [ ] Officers and committees; terms; elections
- [ ] Approval matrix (who can do what, and value limits)
- [ ] Segregation of duties in practice (does one person prepare and approve?)
- [ ] Meeting minutes and resolutions linked to actions
- [ ] AGM: notice, quorum, voting method, minutes ⚖️
- [ ] Internal audit or supervisory committee
- [ ] External audit: auditor appointment, timing, fee, filing with the state ⚖️
- [ ] Annual returns and other filings to the State Department ⚖️
- [ ] Dispute and complaint handling
- [ ] Record retention and archiving ⚖️

## 4.9 Technology and data

- [ ] Every tool used, and which workflow lives where (paper, Excel, WhatsApp, accounting package, cooperative software, bank portal)
- [ ] Where the "source of truth" for balances is, and who holds it
- [ ] Backups: do they exist, and who has copies?
- [ ] Who has access to member data; password sharing
- [ ] Devices used by officers and members; internet reliability; electricity
- [ ] Data volume: members, transactions per month, years of history to migrate
