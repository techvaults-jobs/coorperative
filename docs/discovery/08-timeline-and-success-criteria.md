# 8. Discovery Timeline, Deliverables and Success Criteria

**[RECOMMENDATION]** A 12-week discovery, starting the week of **2 November 2026** and ending **late January 2027**. That is before the NCSR revalidation window is expected to close (about February 2027, if six months from 12 August 2026 holds).

Note: the December and January holiday period will slow access to societies. Book interviews for weeks 3–7 early.

## 8.1 Plan

| Weeks | Phase | Activities | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Set-up and desk research** | Engage counsel and a cooperative auditor; obtain the Act, 3 state laws and the GAID text; competitor desk scan; recruit societies through CFN, alumni, employers and personal networks; finalise consent forms | Verified legal-source pack, recruitment list (target 15 to get 8–12), competitor long-list scores, ethics and consent pack |
| 3–7 | **Field interviews** | 8–12 societies × 3–5 roles (about 35–50 interviews); document collection; observation of a month-end close or deduction reconciliation where possible | Interview snapshots, document intake log, rule-extraction entries |
| 5–8 | **Workflow mapping** | Current-state maps per segment for membership, the deduction cycle, loans, accounting close, exit; competitor demos with the standard scenario | Swimlane workflow diagrams, pain-point heatmap, competitor scorecard |
| 8–10 | **Synthesis** | Requirements matrix; classification into core, configurable, optional, integration, needs-validation; expert reviews (legal, accounting, DPO) | Draft Discovery Report, requirements matrix v1, expert review memos |
| 10–11 | **Concept testing** | Clickable prototypes of 3 critical flows (deduction reconciliation, loan application and approval, exit settlement), tested with 5–8 officers and members | Usability findings, revised flows |
| 12 | **Decision** | Readout to TechVaults leadership; select pilot; agree MVP scope boundaries | **Cooperative Sector Discovery Report**, pilot recommendation, Stage 2 plan |

## 8.2 Team and roles (minimum)

- Research lead (product): owns the plan, interviews, synthesis
- Business analyst: workflows, rule extraction, requirements matrix
- Designer: observation, prototypes, usability tests
- Engineer (part-time): data and integration feasibility, file formats, ledger questions
- Advisers on retainer: cooperative lawyer, cooperative auditor (ICAN), DPO or compliance organisation

## 8.3 Success criteria (discovery is "done" when)

1. **Coverage:** at least 8 societies across at least 3 segments and at least 2 states. At least 3 roles per society on average, including at least 6 treasurers or accountants and at least 4 exited or retired members.
2. **Evidence:** at least 5 societies provided the D-codes marked **Must** in [05](05-sample-collection-template.md), including at least 10 anonymised loan schedules and at least 6 exit settlement sheets.
3. **Rules:** the requirements matrix shows, for each lifecycle area, the observed **range of variation** across societies, with evidence references.
4. **Assumptions:** every row in [07](07-assumptions-register.md) has moved from Untested to a status backed by evidence.
5. **Expert sign-off:** written memos from legal, accounting and data-protection advisers answering [02 §2.6](02-regulatory-landscape-and-sources.md#26-questions-requiring-expert-review), or explicitly deferring each question.
6. **Pain ranking:** the top 10 operational problems ranked by frequency × severity × willingness to pay, each backed by quotes.
7. **Pilot:** at least one society has agreed in writing to pilot, with named champions and data-sharing terms.
8. **Prototype signal:** in usability tests, at least 4 of 5 officers complete the deduction-reconciliation prototype task without help.
