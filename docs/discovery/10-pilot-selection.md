# 10. Choosing the First Pilot Cooperative

## 10.1 Recommendation

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Pilot with **one mid-sized workplace (payroll-deduction) cooperative**: about **300–2,000 members**, a **single employer and payroll source**, and **savings plus 2–4 loan products**. A private-company or university staff cooperative is most likely to fit. Confirm the choice against discovery evidence in week 12.

Why:
- Payroll-deduction societies have the most structured, repeatable monthly cycle. That makes balances easy to verify against existing records. *(Hypothesis A2.)*
- A single payroll source avoids IPPIS or multi-employer complexity in the first release. It still exercises reconciliation, loans, guarantors and exit.
- Mid-size: large enough to feel real pain and pay, small enough to reconcile every member by hand during the pilot.
- University or corporate societies are more likely to have an accountant, an external auditor and decent connectivity. These are the conditions for validating ledger correctness.

**Avoid as the first pilot:** a federal MDA society on IPPIS (integration and bureaucracy risk), a multi-branch multipurpose society (scope), and community or *ajo* groups (different collection model). Study them in discovery; serve them later.

## 10.2 Selection scorecard (score 1–5, weighted)

| Criterion | Weight | What "5" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Fit with the likely MVP segment | 20% | Payroll deductions, savings + loans, single employer |
| Data quality and accessibility | 15% | Electronic records for ≥2 years, reconciled audited accounts, willing to anonymise |
| Committed champions | 15% | Treasurer **and** chairperson actively want change; time allocated |
| Governance approval | 10% | Committee resolution to pilot; clear signatory for data-sharing agreement |
| Representative complexity | 10% | ≥2 loan products, guarantors, some exits per year, dividends |
| Auditor cooperation | 10% | External auditor willing to review pilot outputs |
| Commercial potential | 10% | Willing to convert to paid; referral value to similar societies |
| Logistics | 5% | Reachable for on-site visits; reliable internet |
| Regulatory standing | 5% | Registered and in good standing; revalidation (CVN) in progress |

**Disqualifiers:** an ongoing fraud investigation or major dispute; no audited accounts in the last 2 years; refusal to run parallel operations.

## 10.3 Pilot terms to agree

- Parallel run: the society keeps its current system for ≥3 monthly cycles, and balances are reconciled each month
- Data processing agreement (NDPA-compliant), with controller and processor roles agreed
- Use of anonymised or synthetic data until security review sign-off
- Success metrics: 100% of member balances match after migration (or every difference explained); month-end close time reduced; zero unexplained ledger differences; officer satisfaction
- Exit plan: full data export in open formats if the pilot ends
